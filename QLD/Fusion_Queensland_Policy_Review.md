# Fusion Queensland - Draft Review Register

Version 0.3 follows the user-requested Victorian policy-page layout. This is an editorial review record, not acceptance or endorsement.

## Layout and rule reconciliation

The website format includes The Problem, What it costs, Paid for by, What it does, Research behind this policy, and collapsed Further Detail. Further Detail contains Design Rationale, System Interaction, Economic & Institutional Logic, Risk & Failure Modes, Evidence & Precedent, Implementation Outline, and References.

This deliberately differs from the strict five-tier rules supplied earlier: implementation appears on the page, actions appear under What it does, and contextual material appears outside the tier stack. The user requested the Victorian website layout. Fusion editorial review must determine the publication format; this version does not claim full compliance with the stricter rules.

Cost descriptions identify appraisal requirements rather than verified totals. Paid for by identifies proposed budget routes rather than secured funds. Original action options have been restored as draft proposals and need scope reconciliation against each introductory mechanism. The separate Delivery Plan retains detailed metrics and appraisal tasks.
